Bills below ₹1,000 earn no purchase coins. On an eligible bill, each complete ₹100 earns 1 purchase coin. Coin value is saved when each purchase coin is earned.

Website Coins

Stay signed in with this shop’s website active for more than 2 minutes to earn at most 1 Website Coin per day. Each Website Coin is worth ₹5. Refreshes and extra tabs cannot award more than one daily coin.

Website Coin reward eligibility: 100 Website Coins (₹500 total value), or at least 3 purchase bills above ₹1,000 each. Website Coins stay in your wallet until they are eligible and are never removed when a streak resets.

Wallet and redemption

Your wallet combines Purchase Coins and Website Coins and shows their separate counts and values. Coins are not cash and cannot be converted to cash. Eligible rewards are provided by your connected shop.

Website activity has its own streak. Missing two consecutive days resets the streak only; already earned coins remain in your wallet.
